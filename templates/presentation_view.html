Literature Review & Research Gap Analysis

Surveyed publications from Phase-1 Review Slides 6, 7, and 8

Slides 6-8
No. Paper Title Year / Authors Methodology Observations & Limitations
1 Machine Learning-Based Heart Disease Prediction System 2021
UCI Researchers		 Supervised ML (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees) on clinical data. Single-disease focus; lacks multimodal fusion and SHAP explainability.
2 2D-3D Facial Image Analysis for Feature Identification 2023
Gangothri Sanil et al.		 Deep learning and mesh-based 3D models for feature extraction. High computational demand; 3D data not readily available in clinical screening.
3 Surveillance System for Real-Time Face Recognition 2023
Hyun-Bin Kim et al.		 Deep CNN-based pipeline for real-time video feeds under variable conditions. Struggles with low-light, occlusion; high false positives in noisy input streams.
4 Cross-Domain Person Re-Identification Based on Feature Fusion 2021
Xianjun Luo et al.		 Domain adaptation and attention modules across disparate camera networks. Suffers in unseen domains without adaptation; performance degrades with sensor quality variations.
5 Boosting Deep Feature Fusion for Fake Faces Generated by GANs 2024
Fadwa Alrowais et al.		 Multiple deep CNN feature extraction layers to detect StyleGAN artifacts. Fails against unseen generative models; degrades with compressed low-resolution inputs.
